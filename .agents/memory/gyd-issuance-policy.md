---
name: GYD issuance policy
description: User's supply preference and safeguards needed before adding live GYD minting
---

GYD should have no fixed maximum supply and should mint on demand.

**Why:** the user explicitly chose unlimited, on-demand GYD issuance.

**How to apply:** preserve the no-cap policy. Before connecting minting to live consensus, define the authority, verifiable demand rule, audit evidence, per-operation safeguards, and redemption/burn handling. Do not equate unlimited issuance with a guaranteed USD peg.
