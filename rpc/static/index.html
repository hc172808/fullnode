Guyana's Digital Financial Revolution

Two coins. One mission. A sovereign financial future for every Guyanese.

GYDS Native Utility Coin

Born from Guyana's golden promise, GYDS is the heartbeat of the GYDS Chain — the fuel that powers every transaction, rewards every validator, and secures the network through Proof-of-Stake consensus.

Named for the Guyana Dollar reimagined for the blockchain era, GYDS carries the spirit of a nation rich in natural resources and ambition. Its circuit-board design symbolises Guyana stepping boldly into the digital age — where the rainforest meets the blockchain.

  • ⛏ Validator staking & block rewards
  • ⛽ Gas fees for on-chain transactions
  • 🗳 Governance voting rights
  • 🌐 Cross-chain bridge collateral
GYD Native Stablecoin

GYD is Guyana's answer to financial stability on-chain — a stablecoin pegged 1:1 to the Guyana Dollar (GYD), giving everyday citizens a safe, borderless way to hold value without the volatility of typical crypto assets.

Where GYDS moves markets, GYD anchors commerce. Merchants, remittance senders, and savers across the Caribbean can transact in a familiar unit of account, backed by the transparency of the GYDS blockchain and auditable reserves.

  • 💸 Everyday payments & commerce
  • 📤 Low-cost remittances across borders
  • 🏦 Savings without price volatility
  • 🔄 DeFi liquidity & lending pools
